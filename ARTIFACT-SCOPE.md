# Artifact scope and exclusions

This is a partial-disclosure, anonymous-review artifact. It includes the exact
paper model, executable verification logic, clean and injected L inputs, the
genuine frozen raw observation corpus, complete derivation code, independence
evidence preimages, and the carrier experiment inputs and baselines. It does
not pretend that a frozen-corpus replay is a fresh execution of excluded
engines.

## Included

- The post-notation-fix finite non-laundering model and its report-store, join,
  verifier-scope pin, epoch, and supersession checks.
- The RQ3 envelope, canonicalization, comparison, explicit 160-cell oracle,
  fixture-generation, and fresh-process determinism logic after only the
  mechanical blinding transformations listed in `SOURCE-TRANSFORM.md`.
- Four clean feature programs and six injection programs in DSL L, with the
  stale, claim, outcome, trace, and backend mutations generated by the harness.
- Frozen real observations and per-artifact cryptographic commitments staged by
  the top-level reproduction process.
- The pre-registered carrier protocol, exact final decision subset, separate
  custom-grid and standard-mosaic baseline views, carrier generator, package
  lock, and environment record.

## Excluded, with reasons

1. **E1--E4 implementation source.** The implementation trees are private and
   have no repository-level public license. Their policy permits a specially
   curated public reviewer bundle only with the applicable legal notice, while
   that notice identifies the rights holder and violates this submission's
   absolute blinding rule. The source is therefore excluded rather than
   silently relicensed or incompletely anonymized. E1 and E4 have verified
   blinded source-tree commitments. The genuine E2/E3 run did not capture its
   dirty-tree digest before a concurrent private-worktree rebuild; those source,
   build-profile, and lock commitments are explicitly `UNKNOWN` instead of
   being falsely bound to an earlier or later tree. E2's observed hosted-module
   commitments and both exact executable commitments remain available.
2. **E1--E4 clean and fault-injected binaries.** The native artifacts contain
   embedded project, rights-holder, build-path, and toolchain identifiers.
   Editing those bytes would invalidate the exact executable identities used in
   the experiment. They are excluded; exact byte lengths and SHA-256 values are
   recorded under `engines/e1`--`engines/e4`.
3. **E4 interpreter and generated-native host source closure.** The interpreter
   host belongs to the same private tree. The exact generated-native Rust source
   for the retained E4 binary was not retained as a standalone tracked source
   bundle. Only the generated binary, cross-language evaluator source, and host
   adapter were retained originally; the binary and private sources are bound
   by commitments and excluded. No substitute source was fabricated.
4. **Controlled common-mode fault patch.** The three fault executables were
   retained, but the temporary patch file was not. The original experiment
   record states the operand-specific behavior and restored source digests; it
   does not preserve an exact patch preimage. The excluded fault executables are
   still bound byte-for-byte. No reconstructed patch is presented as original.
5. **Engine dependency caches and build intermediates.** These contain private
   source, absolute paths, timestamps, and identity-bearing metadata and are not
   needed for frozen-corpus verification. Engine lockfiles are excluded with
   the unlicensed engine sources. The experiment-side and carrier dependency
   locks, version facts, and build commands remain included.
6. **Version-control metadata.** Repository names, remotes, commit messages,
   author identities, branch names, and internal revision identifiers are
   stripped. Source/executable SHA-256 commitments replace them as the artifact
   provenance boundary.
7. **Fresh engine execution.** `verify_all.sh --full` regenerates every paper
   number from frozen genuine raw observations but cannot rebuild or rerun the
   excluded engines. It checks canonical and raw byte-identity reruns already
   recorded by fresh processes in that corpus.
8. **Physical print/camera replication.** The carrier study used its declared
   deterministic simulated channel. Independent physical capture is future
   work, not omitted evidence for a claim made by the paper.

These exclusions and the E2/E3 source-preimage race reduce auditability of
engine construction and prevent a from-source engine rerun. They do not change
the finite model checks, oracle
assertions, raw-to-derived computation, or table reproduction. Reviewers should
treat executable/source commitments as provenance evidence, not as a claim of
public source availability.
